# Public Website UX

## Information architecture

Primary navigation:
- Services
- Work
- Approach
- About
- Connect

Additional public routes:
- Contact
- Privacy / Website Use

`/share/` is an operational sensitive-information handoff route and is intentionally excluded from ordinary marketing navigation.

## Primary UX goal

A visitor should quickly understand that Lowcountry Digital Works is an owner-operated practical technology partner for websites, automation, business systems, digital ownership/platform administration, and technical consulting. The experience should demonstrate maintainability, customer ownership, scoped access, evidence, and clear handoff rather than merely state those values.

## Visual character

Preserve **Lowcountry precision** and the locked Tidal Framework brand:
- restrained Estuary Navy / Cypress Teal / Warm Oyster / Soft White palette;
- Manrope-led typography;
- editorial hierarchy before generic card grids;
- real founder/project evidence when public-safe;
- clear status and boundaries for unfinished/internal work.

Avoid generic AI/stock-tech imagery, neon/cyber styling, tourism/beach identity, decorative dashboard UI, excessive gradients, oversized empty heroes, and altered/faded production-logo artwork.

## Homepage flow

1. Editorial hero with owner-operated positioning and Connect/Work actions.
2. Four problem-led service pathways.
3. Selected-work evidence.
4. Customer-ownership principle.
5. Founder trust layer.
6. Assess → Improve or Build → Handoff or Care.
7. Direct Connect CTA.

## Service architecture

First detail routes:
- Websites
- Website Care
- Business Systems & Automation
- Digital Ownership & Platform Administration
- Technology Consulting

Custom applications sit inside Business Systems & Automation unless a future real need justifies a separate public family. Avoid thin SEO service/location pages.

## Work architecture

Project pages show:
- explicit status/type;
- purpose;
- public-safe evidence or screenshot;
- what is actually present;
- boundaries and non-claims;
- related services.

G.A.S. must remain internal service-enabling evidence/measurement infrastructure, never customer SaaS.

## Founder layer

Use Eddie Gugino / Founder. General IT, cybersecurity, and technical-delivery background may be described proportionately, but exact biography, credentials, years, and founder photography require owner approval before production.

## Connect

`/connect/` is the stable LDW-owned QR/business-card destination for phone, text, email, website, verified social profiles, Services, Work, and a portable vCard. It must remain fast, mobile-friendly, and free of a permanent third-party QR dependency.

## Discoverability

Preserve canonicals, page metadata, Open Graph, robots, sitemap, Search Console, and the no-unnecessary-analytics policy. Use visible schema.org microdata where it accurately reflects the page:
- ProfessionalService for the business entity;
- Person for the founder;
- Service for visible service detail;
- BreadcrumbList for real detail hierarchy.

Verified Facebook/X links may serve as `sameAs`. Do not add invisible structured claims, city doorway pages, keyword stuffing, fake blog cadence, testimonials/results, or ranking/revenue guarantees.

## Layout

- max shell width: 1200px;
- readable copy width: ~680–760px;
- wider sections: ~960–1200px;
- gutters: 16px mobile / 24–32px wider screens;
- section spacing: approximately 48–64px mobile / 80–96px desktop;
- breakpoints driven by content.

## Production gate

A technically clean PR and preview are not production approval. Founder photo/biography, exact preview, current → proposed → rollback, and owner approval remain required before production deployment.
