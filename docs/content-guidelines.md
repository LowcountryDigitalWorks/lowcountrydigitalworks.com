# Content Guidelines

Website content should be professional, approachable, specific, and understandable to a small-business owner.

Do not claim:

- a team or staff that does not exist;
- years in operation not supported by the business history;
- named clients without approval;
- 24/7 support;
- guaranteed security, rankings, revenue, or outcomes;
- HIPAA compliance or other formal compliance status without evidence;
- certifications as business credentials when they belong to an individual;
- services that have not been approved.

Explain technical concepts through practical outcomes, ownership, maintainability, security, documentation, and cost control rather than generic technology marketing language.
