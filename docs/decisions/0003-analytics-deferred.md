# ADR 0003: Analytics Deferred

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-08-07

## Decision

Do not add analytics, advertising trackers, or nonessential cookies at launch.

## Rationale

The initial business site does not yet have a demonstrated measurement need that justifies additional privacy, consent, maintenance, and vendor considerations.

## Revisit when

A specific business question cannot be answered through privacy-preserving platform data or other low-impact methods.
